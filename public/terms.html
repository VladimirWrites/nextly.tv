The service

nextly.tv stores an encrypted copy of your library so it can sync between your devices. It is provided free of charge and as-is, with no warranty of any kind — not of availability, accuracy, or fitness for any purpose.

Your account number is your responsibility

Your account number is both your credential and your encryption key. Keep it safe and keep it private. Anyone who has it can read and change your data.

If you lose your account number, your data cannot be recovered — not by you, and not by us. We do not hold a copy of the key and cannot decrypt anything. Export your data regularly; the button is in Settings.

Treat nextly.tv as a convenience, not as an archive of record. We do not guarantee backups, and a bug, an outage, or an account number typed into the wrong place could lose your data. The export file exists precisely so you can hold your own copy.
